# Quicksilver Model v1.1
_Last updated: August 31st 2026_

This version allows Quicksilver to detect AI music generated by Suno, Udio, Riffusion, and Mureka.

# Dataset Breakdown

**AI audio sources**
* 30s random snippets of Suno songs
* 30s random snippets of Udio songs
* 30s random snippets of Riffusion songs
* 30s random snippets of Mureka songs

**Human audio sources**
* 30s clips from FMA medium dataset (human music)
* 30s clips from MTG-Jamendo dataset (human music)
* 5-10s clips from Audioset (random human audio from YouTube)
* 5-30s clips from Mozilla's Common Voice Spontaneous Speech 3.0 (English)
